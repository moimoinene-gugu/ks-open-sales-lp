SERVICE TERMS

Service Terms

These terms outline the general conditions that apply to Ks Open. landing page projects. Project-specific details are confirmed separately in the applicable service agreement.

Service Scope

Ks Open. provides custom landing page design and development services based on the agreed project scope. The Founding Client Launch Program includes one custom landing page, responsive design, basic messaging and content structure support, CTA and inquiry form setup, image and content placement, basic launch support, and up to two revision rounds.

Project Information and Materials

Clients are responsible for providing the information, content, images, access details, and other materials reasonably required to complete the project.

Project Timeline

The project timeline begins once the required deposit and all necessary project information and materials have been received. A typical landing page project is completed within approximately 7–10 business days, subject to the agreed scope, client response times, and requested revisions.

Payment

For the Founding Client Launch Program, the total project price is CAD $295.

A 50% deposit is required before work begins. The remaining 50% is due before launch.

Standard card processing fees are included in the displayed price and are covered by Ks Open. Payments are processed securely through Stripe.

Cancellation Before Work Begins

If the client cancels before work begins, payments received will be refunded less any payment processing fees that were incurred and are not returned to Ks Open. by the payment provider.

Cancellation After Work Begins

Once work has begun, the deposit is generally non-refundable because time and resources have already been committed to the project.

Ks Open. may, at its discretion, consider the amount of work completed and any exceptional circumstances when determining whether any partial refund is appropriate.

Cancellation by Ks Open.

If Ks Open. is unable to continue providing the agreed service, any refund will be based on the portion of the service that has not yet been provided.

Completed or Launched Projects

Refunds are generally not available for client-requested cancellations after the project has been completed, approved, or launched.

Any issues resulting from Ks Open.'s failure to provide the agreed service will be addressed in accordance with the applicable service agreement.

Revisions

The Founding Client Launch Program includes up to two revision rounds. Additional revisions or work outside the agreed scope may require a separate estimate and client approval.

Client Approval

The client is responsible for reviewing and approving project content, design, and other deliverables before launch.

Third-Party Services

Some projects may involve third-party platforms, hosting, domains, payment providers, form services, or other external tools. Ks Open. is not responsible for outages, policy changes, service limitations, or other issues caused by third-party providers outside its reasonable control.

Project-Specific Agreement

These Service Terms provide general information. The scope, deliverables, responsibilities, payment terms, timeline, and other project-specific conditions are confirmed in the Client Service Agreement provided before work begins.

Questions
