PRIVACY POLICY

When you contact Ks Open. or inquire about a project, we may collect information such as your name, business name, email address, project details, and other information needed to provide our services.

We use the information we collect to respond to inquiries, communicate with you, prepare and manage projects, provide contracted services, improve our services, and carry out related business activities.

Payments are processed through Stripe. Credit card information is handled through Stripe's secure systems, and Ks Open. does not directly store or manage your full card details.

Service Providers

Ks Open. may use third-party service providers for functions such as payment processing, form submissions, website hosting, communications, and other business operations. These providers handle information according to their own privacy and security practices.

Cookies and Website Analytics

This website may use cookies or analytics tools to understand website usage and improve the visitor experience. Information collected through these tools generally does not directly identify you by name.

Safeguarding Information

Ks Open. takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Access, Correction, and Deletion

If you would like to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you, please contact Ks Open. through the website contact form.

Privacy Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of personal information can be submitted through the Ks Open. contact form.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to our services, practices, or legal requirements.
